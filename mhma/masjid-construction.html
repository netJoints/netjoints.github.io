The Vision

A Permanent Home for MHMA

مَنْ بَنَى مَسْجِدًا لِلَّهِ بَنَى اللَّهُ لَهُ مِثْلَهُ فِي الْجَنَّةِ

"Whoever builds a masjid for Allah, Allah will build for him its like in Paradise." — Sahih Muslim

Since our founding in 2010, MHMA has served the Muslim community of Mountain House — but always from rented and temporary spaces. Today we are working to build what our community deserves: a purpose-built Islamic Center that will serve our families as a center of worship, education, and belonging.

The Islamic Center of Mountain House (ICMH) will be a landmark we are proud to pass on to our children — a masjid that is always open, always welcoming, and always a source of barakah for our entire community.

What We're Building

Planned Facilities

A comprehensive Islamic center designed to serve every member of our community — from the youngest children to our elders.

🕌

Prayer Hall

A purpose-built, spacious prayer hall with dedicated men's and women's sections — designed for daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and large Eid gatherings of the whole community.

📚

Maktab & Classrooms

A dedicated Islamic learning center with fully equipped classrooms for Quran, Arabic, Sunday School, Hifz, and adult education programs.

⚽

Sports Facility

A community sports facility providing space for basketball, fitness, and youth recreation — promoting well-being and brotherhood alongside worship.

🤝

Community Center

A flexible multipurpose hall for community events, fundraisers, community dinners, lectures, and interfaith engagement.

🌿

Outdoor Spaces

Landscaped gathering areas, outdoor space for youth and families, and ample parking to accommodate our growing congregation comfortably.

♿

Full Accessibility

Designed from the ground up for full accessibility — so every member of our community, regardless of ability, can participate and worship with ease.
