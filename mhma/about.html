Who We Are

The Mountain House Muslim Association (MHMA) was founded in 2010 by a small group of families committed to building a spiritual home for the growing Muslim community of Mountain House, California.

Today we serve over 500 families — offering daily prayers, Islamic education, social services, and community programs that span all ages and backgrounds.

Our mission is simple: to create a welcoming, vibrant masjid that serves as the spiritual and community center for Muslims in the Tri-Valley area.
