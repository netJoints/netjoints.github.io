# AgenticOps Product Leadership

My current work at Cisco sits at the intersection of AgenticOps product management, AI-assisted network operations, cloud-managed switching, and customer-centered technical strategy.

## Leadership principles

- Start with the operator or customer pain point and make the outcome measurable.
- Connect product direction to architecture, evidence, and a practical path to adoption.
- Use AI to reduce toil and improve decisions while keeping humans accountable for consequential actions.
- Treat security, privacy, observability, and rollback as product qualities—not afterthoughts.
- Build reusable technical patterns that help teams move from a compelling demo to dependable operations.
