Agentic AI Travel Agency

A travel-planning experiment showing how specialized agents can coordinate recommendations, constraints, and service integration.

Pain point

Travel planning combines loosely structured preferences, changing availability, price constraints, and multiple service calls.

Solution

Use an orchestrator to decompose the request, call bounded provider tools, compare options, and present a reviewable itinerary.
