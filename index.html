About Shahzad Ali

Startup Grit.

I'm Shahzad Ali — a leader who builds and runs highly effective teams across security, cloud, and Agentic AI. Currently leading Agentic Operations & Management (AgenticOps) product strategy at Cisco Systems, embedding governed, policy-bound AI agents directly into enterprise network operations.

I move comfortably between a scrappy startup — hands-on, shipping, always learning — and a global enterprise, where I've led solutions architecture, pre-sales, and product teams that win business at scale. Most recently I led cybersecurity pre-sales solutions architecture at Britive, and before that spent five years as VP Solutions Architecture at Aviatrix, where I drove 7x ARR growth ($9M → $68M), founded the Aviatrix Certified Engineer (ACE) Program — the industry's first multicloud networking certification — and was recognized as the "Father of Multicloud Network Architecture (MCNA)."

Thirteen years at Cisco Systems and leading VMware's Networking and Security Solutions Architecture team built the technical foundation — dual CCIE, VCDX, and certifications across AWS, Azure, and GCP.
