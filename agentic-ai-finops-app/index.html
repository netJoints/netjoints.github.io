Agentic AI FinOps Initiative

An enterprise FinOps prototype that combined multiple AI agents with just-in-time privileged access to help users obtain cost and operational insight securely and automatically.

Open source repositoryAI Projects

Leadership context

I initiated and led this prototype while leading the sales engineering team at a Cloud PAM startup. The work was designed for an enterprise customer and helped my team demonstrate how secure access, AI orchestration, and FinOps could become a practical customer solution.

Business and team outcome

The initiative focused on increasing employee productivity, improving the help provided to field and partner teams, and turning a technically differentiated prototype into customer value that supported a successful enterprise opportunity.

Pain point

FinOps users need timely answers about cloud cost and usage, but investigation often requires multiple systems, specialist knowledge, and privileged credentials that should not remain active.

Solution

A supervisor agent receives the request, understands the intent, and delegates to specialized compliance and FinOps agents. JIT PAM checks out short-lived credentials only for the approved task, then the workflow returns an explainable answer to the user.

Architecture and agent workflow

  1. User request and identity context
  2. Supervisor agent routes the task
  3. Compliance agent validates policy and access
  4. FinOps agent retrieves and explains cost data

The supervisor coordinates the agents; compliance constrains the action; the FinOps specialist gathers the relevant information; and the response layer presents the result with enough context for a human to review it.

AI and AgenticOps

AI handled request interpretation, delegation, and explanation. AgenticOps supplied the operating model: scoped access, approval boundaries, evidence capture, failure handling, and a clear human escalation path.

Leadership principles demonstrated

This initiative connected technical excellence to measurable operating outcomes: less manual investigation, faster enablement for employees and field partners, safer access to enterprise data, and a stronger customer story for the sales engineering team.
