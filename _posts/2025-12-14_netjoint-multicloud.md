Networking and Networks have transformed over period of time. Enterprises have realized that public cloud is the strategic direction for their IT infrastructure and applications. The cloud service providers (CSPs) like Amazon AWS, Google GCP and Microsoft Azure are extremely efficient at providing networking, security, compute and storage capabilities in their respective public cloud such as AWS, GCP and Azure.

“All Clouds are not created equally”. In order to get the best of each and every cloud, there is a need to create seamless joints between those clouds which should, like human body joints, work in conjunction with each others. This joining and marriage has given birth to a new Security and Networking Architecture, what we call “Multicloud” today. Multicloud is an umbrella term used for public, private and hybrid clouds.
