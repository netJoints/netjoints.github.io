**SAP S4/HANA supports the integration with other SAP and 3rd party products. Few examples**

  * Ariba Network (procurement related) 
    * Identify sources of supply for purchase requisitions in SAP S4/HANA. Once suppliers are registered on Ariba Network, SAP S4/HANA offers the seamless purchase and invoice collaboration processes with the suppliers.
  * SAP Ariba (procurement related)
  * SAP Fieldglass (sales related)
  * SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central (internal employee portal)
  * SAP Concur Solutions (employee travel)
  * SAP Global Trade Services
  * SAP Manufacturing Suite
  * Industry Cloud Solutions
