## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think, learn, and perform tasks that typically require human cognition.

At its core, AI involves creating algorithms and developing systems that can process data, recognize patterns, make decisions, and improve over time through experience.

AI = Machines act like humans.

**Narrow AI:** Perform a single task also called Weak AI. Such as playing a Go game. Here the computer does one thing very well.  
  
**Multi-modality AI** = AGI = Artificial General Intelligence: Machines doing multiple things very well. More like humans. 

## Key Concepts and Components of AI

### Machine Learning (ML)

  * A subset of AI focused on building systems to learn from and make data-based decisions.
  * Techniques like supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning fall under this category.



### Deep Learning (Neural Networks)

  * It enables machines to understand, interpret, and respond to human language.
  * They are used in chatbots, sentiment analysis, and language translation applications.
