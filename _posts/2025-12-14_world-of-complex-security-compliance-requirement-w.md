## DORA

The **Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)** focuses on strengthening the IT security and operational resilience of financial entities in the European Union. Britive’s software, which specializes in **multi-cloud privileged access management** , offers several features that can support compliance with DORA’s requirements:

### 1\. **Permissions for Secure Access**

**DORA Alignment:** Britive’s platform provides just-in-time (JIT) privileged access to critical systems. It reduces the risk of unauthorized access or privilege misuse, a key element of operational resilience.

### 2\. Centralized Audit Logging and Monitoring

**DORA Alignment:** Britive platform logs all access activity across cloud environments. It supports the requirement to log and monitor all IT activities for accountability and incident response.

### 3\. **Risk-Based Access Controls**

**DORA Alignment:** Britive supports adaptive access controls based on contextual factors such as user location, device, and behavior. This meets the DORA requirement of operational resilience.

### 4\. **Compliance and Audit Reporting**

**DORA Alignment:** Britive provides detailed compliance reporting capabilities for auditing purposes. It aligns with DORA’s regular reporting and evidence gathering requirement. 

### 5\. **Incident Response and Forensics Support**

**DORA Alignment:** Britive tracks and logs privileged access events, providing forensic insights. It aligns with swift investigation and resolution of IT disruptions or cyberattacks, as mandated by DORA.
