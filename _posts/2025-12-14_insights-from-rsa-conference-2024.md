Recently, I attended the RSA Conference 2024, a premier gathering showcasing the most innovative cybersecurity advancements. There was a [broad spectrum of companies](https://www.rsaconference.com/usa/expo-and-sponsors#sort=%40name%20ascending&numberOfResults=100), from application security, endpoint security, user-to-service security, and storage security. Almost all the vendors were hailing from a traditional on-premises background. Per my observation, they showcased the solution for the last-generation on-prem world. Aviatrix led the discussion by showcasing solutions to business issues related to network modernization with its cloud, multicloud, and hybrid cloud secure networking platform. 

In a nutshell, Aviatrix distinguished itself as the pioneer with a unique value proposition for secure cloud networking and distributed cloud firewalls. We’re not just another vendor but the secure networking co-pilot in the enterprise journey to protect and secure business assets while reducing costs.

## Elevating Cloud Security with Aviatrix Security Training and Certification

The pressing need to bridge the cloud security gap is not just a challenge; it’s an opportunity for innovation and transformation. My dialogue with numerous RSA conference attendees underscored the significance of the Aviatrix ACE Program. The Aviatrix Certified Engineer (ACE) Program was praised as a pivotal solution, specifically the [ACE Security Certification](https://aviatrix.com/ace-security/).

### ACE Security Certification Course

The enthusiasm for the Aviatrix ACE Security course validated our approach—education and certification that address real-world challenges and enable real-world successes. 

The ACE Security track equips professionals with the necessary knowledge and skills to navigate the complexities of cloud security, ensuring that organizations can fully leverage the cloud’s potential in a secure, agile, and cost-effective manner. This program isn’t just another training; it’s a comprehensive pathway to mastering cloud, multicloud, and hybrid cloud security, a crucial need identified by conference attendees.

In our discussions, it was pleasing to hear that we’re already sculpting the future with the ACE Security certification. 

**Why?**

Because at its core, the ACE Security certification encapsulates a deep dive into the nuances of cloud security, arming professionals to tackle the challenges they face in the cloud-native, multicloud, and hybrid cloud. It’s not about learning from textbooks; it’s about getting your hands dirty with real-world scenarios. 

I’m pumped about the potential this unveils for us and the industry at large. It’s not just about staying ahead of the curve but setting it.
