Cisco AgenticOps

AgenticOps turns AI from advice into governed action

AgenticOps uses AI agents that understand goals, reason through problems, coordinate tasks, and act through approved workflows while humans stay in control.

  • Moves beyond alerts and recommendations toward end-to-end operational workflows.
  • Combines telemetry, reasoning, orchestration, policy, and audit trails in one shared workspace.
  • Supports NetOps and SecOps teams with supervised autonomy instead of unchecked automation.
